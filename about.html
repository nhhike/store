About Me

I’m a passionate explorer of both the natural world and the frontiers of technology — an Electrical and Electronics Engineer by training and trade, with a deep love for capturing life’s most breathtaking moments through the lens.

Engineering Mindset: As an EEE Engineer, I thrive on solving complex problems, designing innovative systems, and pushing the boundaries of what’s possible with electricity, electronics, and embedded systems. My analytical approach shapes how I see and interact with the world around me.

Visual Storyteller: I’m an avid cinematographer and photographer, constantly chasing golden hour light, epic landscapes, and authentic human stories. Whether it’s behind the camera on a film set or deep in the wilderness with my gear, I live to freeze time and tell compelling visual narratives.

Outdoor Enthusiast: Nature is my playground and my sanctuary. I’m a dedicated hiker, backpacker, and angler who finds peace and purpose on remote trails, mountain summits, and quiet fishing spots. From multi-day backpacking expeditions to sunrise summits, the outdoors fuels my creativity and restores my spirit.

Adventure Seeker: When I’m not on foot, you’ll find me mountain biking technical trails, rockhounding for hidden geological treasures, or off-roading in my Jeep through rugged backcountry roads. I live for the thrill of discovery — whether it’s a new trail, a rare mineral, or an untouched campsite under the stars.

Curious Truth-Seeker: I’m a validated conspiracy theorist who questions mainstream narratives and digs deeper into hidden patterns, power structures, and unconventional ideas. My engineering background gives me a sharp, logical lens through which I analyze information and challenge assumptions.

Prompt Engineer Extraordinaire: I speak fluent Prompt — expertly crafting precise, creative instructions for AI systems to generate everything from stunning visuals to complex code and deep insights. It’s a modern superpower that blends my technical expertise with creative vision.

Whether I’m engineering the future, framing the perfect shot, or chasing the next adventure in the wild, I bring the same curiosity, precision, and passion to everything I do. Welcome to my corner of the internet — let’s explore, create, and question together.
