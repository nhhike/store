The 10 Essentials for NH Hiking

When heading out into the White Mountains or any NH trail, preparation is key. Here are the items you should always have in your pack:

  • 1. Navigation Map, compass, and GPS system (like HikerLog). Know how to use them!
  • 2. Sun Protection Sunscreen, sunglasses, and sun-protective clothing.
  • 3. Insulation Extra layers for sudden weather changes (jacket, hat, gloves).
  • 4. Illumination Headlamp or flashlight with extra batteries.
  • 5. First-Aid Supplies Including foot care and insect repellent.
  • 6. Fire Matches, lighter, and fire starter.
